# Side Quests & Hooks

Optional content that deepens Oz without gating the critical path. **Cut list priority: low.**

## Side Quests

| # | Name | Giver | Hook | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | **The Baker's Ledger** | Munchkin baker | Recover debt records from the East witch's vault | Basket capacity +1 |
| S2 | **Oil for the Weeping Man** | Winkie widow | Find her husband's oil can before the rust takes him | Tin Man kit upgrade |
| S3 | **The Coward's Mirror** | Lion (optional dialogue) | Find a mirror that shows courage; Lion refuses to look | Courage meter capacity |
| S4 | **Crows at the Chapel** | Gillikin priest | Clear crows from a ruined chapel; they're guarding something | Lore: Ozma's lineage |
| S5 | **The Last Munchkin** | Rogue defector | Escort a defector out without killing his brothers | Emerald + cosmetic |
| S6 | **Pages of Baum** (collectible) | Hidden | 12 lore pages scattered across Oz | Art gallery / lore codex |
| S7 | **Kalidah Hunt** | Wilds hunter | Track and defeat the Kalidah | Shoe cosmetic |
| S8 | **The Wizard's Mail** | Intercepted letters | Deliver (or burn) letters that reveal the regime's lies | Story branch flavor |

## Collectibles

- **Pages of Baum** — 12 lore pages; each a short in-world excerpt or illustration.
- **Munchkin trinkets** — 40 small curios; basket flavor text.
- **Shoe polish** — cosmetic variants (no stats).

## Challenge Shrines

Optional combat trials (3 per region) that test a specific kit:

| Shrine | Test |
| --- | --- |
| Shrine of Straw | Survive waves with Scarecrow only |
| Shrine of Tin | Break armor under time pressure |
| Shrine of Fur | Stealth past a patrol without being seen |
| Shrine of Heels | Blink-only obstacle course |

Rewards: cosmetics and lore, **never power**.

## Design Rules

- No side quest requires a companion the player hasn't met.
- No side quest gates the main path.
- Every side quest is completable in one session (≤20 min).
- Side quests use existing assets/enemies wherever possible.
