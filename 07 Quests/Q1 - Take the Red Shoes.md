# Q1 — Take the Red Shoes

> **Founder quest:** “Take the red shoes. (Dodge/kick ability only.)”

## Objective

Start in Kansas, survive the tornado inside the house, steer the house onto the East Witch, and claim the Shoes.

## Beats

0. **Kansas (cutscene, black and white)** — the Gale farm; the sky turns; Dorothy runs for the house. Quiet, sparse, storm-driven storytelling.
1. **The Canvas (playable, black and white)** — Dorothy starts on a canvas/rug inside the house. Debris breaks in; dodge falling objects and **close the open window** to stop the wind. First movement lesson.
2. **Lift-Off (black and white)** — the house tears free; the room tilts and becomes a physics playground.
3. **The Flight (playable, black and white)** — the house is mid-air. **Steer** left/right and avoid obstacles (trees, livestock, roof beams, another farmhouse); a soft aim reticle shows the landing zone.
4. **Impact (color)** — the house lands directly on the **Wicked Witch of the East**; the world floods with color as Munchkinland appears.
5. **The Curse** — her dying magic animates debris and munchkin puppets; Dorothy can only dodge and kick.
6. **The Claim** — the Shoes choose Dorothy; the curse snaps.
7. **The Aftermath** — the Lollipop Guild cautiously approaches; their leader asks for help.

## Mechanics Taught

| Beat | Verb |
| --- | --- |
| Canvas | Walk/run, interact (close window), dodge debris |
| Flight | Steer, avoid obstacles, aim the landing |
| Impact | Context: land on the Witch |
| Curse attacks | **Blink** (dodge) |
| Puppet legs | **Kick** (stagger/launch) |
| Escape the wreck | Long teleport (first taste, short range) |

## Prologue Rules

- Kansas through impact is **black and white**; color returns on landing.
- The interior is a contained tutorial room: no combat, no death (debris knocks Dorothy down and resets her to the last safe spot).
- Flight is short (~60–90 s): forgiving steering, clear obstacles, one "perfect landing" cosmetic.
- The landing is **player-aimed**, not automatic — the reticle makes the Witch the obvious target.
- If the player misses, the house still lands in Munchkinland and the Witch is still crushed (the story holds); only the perfect-landing cosmetic is lost. **Never fail the player out of the premise.**

## Combat Rules (this quest only)

- **No summons.** No companions yet.
- **No weapons.** Kick only.
- Dorothy cannot die permanently; failures reset the beat.

## Enemies

- Storm debris and household objects (interior)
- Flying obstacles and livestock silhouettes (flight)
- Enchanted debris (destructible)
- Munchkin puppets (kick fodder)
- The cursed legs (mini-phase)

## Boss

[[Witch of the East]] — playable prologue + cinematic set piece (12–16 min).

## Rewards

- **The Shoes** (blink + short teleport).
- First lore items: debt ledger, propaganda poster (basket).
- Quest hook → [[Q2 - The Lollipop Guild]].

## Writing Hooks

- Dorothy: “I just want to go home.”
- Munchkin elder: “You killed her. …Oh, thank goodness.”
- Locasta (off-screen voice): “Those shoes were never hers to keep.”

## Success Criteria

Player can close the window, steer the house onto the Witch, then reliably blink through a telegraphed attack and kick a puppet off a ledge before the quest ends.
