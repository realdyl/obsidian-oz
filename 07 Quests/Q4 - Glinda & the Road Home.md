# Q4 — Glinda & the Road Home

> **Founder quest:** “Locate Glinda Goode (Good Witch of the South).”

## Prototype (L5) — `oz/regions/quadling/glinda_court.*`

YBR south → Glinda multi-line E counsel (Shoes always home; balloon is the **Wizard's** road) → **FINAL STEP** pink Road Home pad · **E ×3 heel clicks** → hourglass → **L6** [[L6 Kansas Homecoming]]. F8 skip counsel · F9 skip to homecoming. Whimsy palette `glinda`. Multi-ending table below remains design scope; prototype ships **go home** path only.

## Prototype (L6) — `oz/regions/kansas/homecoming.*`

B&W interactive finale (mirror L1): land → Aunt Em E lines → porch FINAL STEP E → epilogue. See [[L6 Kansas Homecoming]].

## Objective

Reach Glinda, learn what the Shoes are for, confront the Wizard, and choose a home.

## Beats

1. **The Emerald City** — spectacle, propaganda, guards, hidden machinery.
2. **The Wizard's Audience** — the great head; the lie.
3. **The Machine** — boss P1–P2 ([[The Wizard (Boss)]]).
4. **Behind the Curtain** — the man; the choice.
5. **Glinda's Court (South)** — she reveals the Shoes were always a key home.
6. **The Road Home** — epilogue; Ozma restored; companions honored.

## Structure Note

The founder notes place Glinda *after* the Wizard's defeat as the final guide. We honor that: **Glinda is the last quest-giver**, not a mid-game exposition dump.

## Enemies

- Emerald City Guard (shield walls)
- Flying Monkeys (defecting, then hostile holdouts)
- Mechanical constructs (Wizard's machine)
- Illusion clones of companions

## Boss — The Wizard

See [[The Wizard (Boss)]]. Phases: **Spectacle → Machine → Man**.

## The Choice

| Choice | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| **Go home** | Shoes open the road; Oz left to Ozma; bittersweet |
| **Stay / give the Shoes to Ozma** | Dorothy chooses Oz; warmer, ambiguous |
| **Take the machine** | Secret “Wicked” ending; Dorothy becomes the next Wizard |

## Rewards

- Ending cinematic (per choice).
- Gallery unlock.
- New Game+ (optional).

## Writing Hooks

- Glinda: “You have had the way home on your feet since the day you landed. You needed to be *ready* to use it.”
- Ozma: “Oz remembers.”
- Toto: (barks once; the road opens)

## Success Criteria

Player understands, before Glinda explains it, that the Shoes are the key — the reveal should feel inevitable, not arbitrary.
