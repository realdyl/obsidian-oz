# Q2 — The Lollipop Guild

> **Founder quest:** “Locate the Lollipop Guild (quest to wipe out rogue munchkins siding with Witch of East, then on to Locasta, Witch of the North). This is where you find Scarecrow, right outside the fields.”

## Objective

Find the Lollipop Guild, break the rogue munchkin hold on Munchkinland, and reach Locasta in the North.

## Beats

1. **The Guild Hall** — a candy-colored square gone sour; collaborators run protection rackets.
2. **Three Camps** — clear rogue munchkin camps (swarm combat; no companion yet → shoes + kick).
3. **The Marshal** — rogue munchkin leader empowered by East-witch relics.
4. **The Fields** — the road north; a Scarecrow impaled on a pole warns travelers away.
5. **Free the Scarecrow** — he joins; his kit unlocks.
6. **Locasta** — she explains Ozma, Mombi, and the Wizard's lie.

## Mechanics Taught

| Beat | Verb/Kit |
| --- | --- |
| Camps | Crowd management with kick + blink |
| Traps | Blink through, kick to disarm |
| Marshal boss | **Scarecrow**: Crows and Confuse |
| Fields | Companion command (follow/hold) |

## Enemies

- Rogue Munchkins (swarm, slings, snares)
- Living Trees (ambush on the road)
- Elite: Rogue Munchkin Slinger

## Boss — Rogue Munchkin Marshal *(proposed)*

- **Arena:** Guild square, candy stalls as cover.
- **P1:** adds from stalls; Marshal hangs back with a relic sling.
- **P2:** Marshal buffs adds; **Confuse** turns them on each other.
- **P3:** Marshal uses an East-witch curse; **Crows** interrupt the cast.
- **Purpose:** teach that the newest friend is the answer, not raw damage.

> [!question] Naming
> “Rogue Munchkin Marshal” is a placeholder. Alternatives: *The Gumdrop Golem*, *The Licorice Sheriff*, *The Marshal of Sweets*. See [[Open Questions]].

## Rewards

- **Scarecrow** joins.
- Guild becomes a safe hub (shop, fast-travel shrine).
- Shoe Tier 2 materials.
- Lead → [[Q3 - Locasta & the Princess]].

## Writing Hooks

- Scarecrow: “I'm not much of a fighter. I'm more of a… strongly worded suggestion.”
- Locasta: “The Wizard didn't save Oz. He rented it.”
- Munchkin child: “Are you the one who dropped the house?”

## Success Criteria

Player uses Confuse at least once to solve the Marshal's add phase without being told.
