# Q3 — Locasta & the Princess

> **Founder quest:** “Locasta protects the Princess, she needs you to clear the path and take out Mombi who has her trapped as a young boy (Tip). This is where you get the lion. He's among the lions, tigers, and bears.”

## Objective

Clear the northern road, recruit the Lion in the Wilds, assault Mombi's manor, and free Ozma.

## Beats

1. **Locasta's Court** — Locasta reveals Ozma's fate: hidden by Mombi as a boy called **Tip**.
2. **The Gillikin Road** — clear blocked passes (blink + crows + kick-launch puzzles).
3. **The Wilds** — lions, tigers, and bears; a **frightened Lion** hides from everything.
4. **Trust Encounter** — no fight; Dorothy calms the Lion (stealth approach, no summons near him).
5. **Mombi's Manor** — stitched servants, storm traps, the cage-lantern.
6. **Mombi** — boss (see [[Mombi]]).
7. **The Reveal** — Tip is Ozma; Mombi's hold breaks; Ozma blesses the companions.

## Mechanics Taught

| Beat | Verb/Kit |
| --- | --- |
| Road puzzles | Long teleport, Crows on switches |
| Wilds | Stealth approach; **Lion restriction** (can't summon near enemies) |
| Manor | Roar interrupts; Cloak for repositioning |
| Mombi | Seam targeting; protect Ozma |

## Enemies

- Mangled Lions, Tigers, Bears
- Living Trees
- Stitched servants (Mombi's adds)
- Flying Monkey scouts (first appearance, foreshadowing West)

## Companion Acquisition

**Lion** is found among the Lions, Tigers & Bears, not in a boss arena. He joins after a **trust encounter**:

- Approach slowly, dismiss Scarecrow, no weapons.
- Toto helps (sniffs him out).
- He follows at a distance until Mombi's storm forces him to act.
- His first Roar is scripted and saves Dorothy — then he's playable.

## Boss — Mombi

See [[Mombi]] for full phases. Key: **Roar interrupts the storm**, **Cloak** enables seam hits, **axe** severs stitches.

## Rewards

- **Lion** joins.
- **Ozma freed** (endgame legitimacy).
- Companion meter capacity +1 (Ozma's blessing).
- Lead → Winkie Country ([[Witch of the West]]).

## Writing Hooks

- Locasta: “She has been a boy for eleven years. He does not remember being a princess.”
- Lion: “I'm not scared of you. I'm scared of *everything else*.”
- Ozma: “You came for a boy and found a queen. I'm sorry it took so long.”

## Success Criteria

Player discovers the Lion restriction organically (UI explains it once, clearly) and uses Roar to interrupt Mombi at least twice.
