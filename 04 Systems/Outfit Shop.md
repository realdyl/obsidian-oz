# Outfit Shop

The Outfit Shop is the primary hub for character customization and the expression of the game's "Hedonistic Horror" aesthetic. It is managed by **The Tailor of Vices**, an enigmatic figure who trades in the currency of longing.

## The Currency: Desire Fragments

Instead of traditional money, the shop uses **Desire Fragments**—crystalline shards of concentrated longing extracted from the inhabitants of Oz.

### How to Earn Fragments
- **Seductive Combat**: Bonus fragments are awarded when enemies are defeated using "Allure" moves or manipulated into submission.
- **Forbidden Trinkets**: Collectibles hidden in the world, often found in secret "Erotic Shrines" or hidden alcoves.
- **Boss Essence**: Defeating a companion boss grants a large sum of fragments, representing the liberation of that companion's suppressed desires.
- **Vile Requests**: Completing side quests for NPCs that involve satisfying specific, kinky, or taboo requests.

## The Shop Logic
The shop is not a static menu but an interactive experience. The Tailor offers different "Collections" based on the player's progress and the regions they have unlocked.

### Collection Types
- **The Gingham Collection**: Variations on Dorothy's classic look, shifting from innocent to provocative.
- **The Burlap & Leather Collection**: Focused on the Scarecrow's puppet-like aesthetic and leather restraints.
- **The Chrome & Velvet Collection**: Focused on the Tin Man's polished industrialism and erotic textures.
- **The Primal Gold Collection**: Focused on the Lion's raw power and regal, animalistic harnesses.

## Customization & Impact
Outfits are not merely cosmetic; they influence how the world perceives the characters.
- **Allure Multiplier**: Certain outfits increase the effectiveness of "Allure" moves against specific enemy types.
- **Intimidation**: More aggressive, kinky attire can intimidate weaker enemies, causing them to flee or hesitate.
- **Visual Progression**: The player's attire serves as a visual record of their descent into (or mastery of) the hedonism of Oz.
