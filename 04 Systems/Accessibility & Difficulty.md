# Accessibility & Difficulty

Accessibility is a **pillar-level requirement**, not a menu afterthought. The game is about borrowing strength — the options should let players borrow the right kind of help.

## Difficulty Presets

| Preset | Dorothy health | Enemy damage | Telegraph length | Aim assist | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Story** | 1.5× | 0.5× | 1.3× | Strong | For narrative players |
| **Balanced** | 1× | 1× | 1× | Standard | Default |
| **Wicked** | 0.75× | 1.5× | 0.85× | Off | For action veterans |

Presets are **fully customizable** after selection; presets are just starting points.

## Assist Options

- **Blink i-frame window** slider (wider).
- **Companion auto-cast** (AI uses abilities on cooldown).
- **Auto-waypoint** (replaces Toto trail).
- **Aim assist** for long teleport.
- **Hold vs. toggle** for all holds.
- **Combat speed** (0.7×–1.0×).
- **Boss phase checkpoints** on retry.
- **Skip boss after N deaths** (with story summary) — optional, off by default.

## Input & Motor

- Full remapping (keyboard/mouse and controller).
- One-handed layouts.
- No mandatory rapid-tap or simultaneous-stick prompts.
- Adjustable dead zones, sensitivity, inversion.
- Subtitles for all dialogue, size/background/color options.
- Speaker labels and directional captions for important sounds.

## Visual

- **Colorblind-safe telegraphs:** shape + motion + color (never color alone).
- High-contrast mode for UI and interactables.
- UI scale 80–150%.
- Reduce/disable screen shake, camera bob, flash, and chromatic effects.
- Motion-sickness options: FOV, camera smoothing, vignette, motion blur off.

## Cognitive

- Quest log with plain-language summaries.
- “What was I doing?” recap on load.
- Optional persistent objective banner.
- Puzzle hints escalate on repeated failure.
- No timed dialogue choices.

## Audio

- Separate sliders: master, music, SFX, voice, UI.
- Mono audio option.
- Visual cues for off-screen threats.

## Testing Plan

- Playtest with at least 3 accessibility profiles before content lock.
- Verify every telegraph has a non-color tell.
- Verify all critical path guidance works with Toto hints disabled.
