# Progression & Upgrades

## Philosophy

**Ability-gated, not stat-gated.** The player gets *new verbs and better versions of verbs*, not +5% damage. No XP bars, no level-ups, no loot rarity.

## The Spine

| Gate | Unlock | What it opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Act 1 | Silver Shoes | Blink + teleport; tutorial combat |
| Act 1 boss | Scarecrow | Crowd control; first real tactical choice |
| Act 2 | Shoe Tier 2 | Longer teleports; Wilds access |
| Act 2 boss | Lion + Ozma | Stealth/fear; stealth routes |
| Act 3 | Shoe Tier 3; Tin Man | Armor breaking; hazard traversal; Winkie Country |
| Act 3 boss | Shoe Tier 4 | Chain blink; Emerald City |
| Act 4 | All kits | Finale |

## Currency

**Emeralds** (soft currency) drop from elites, bosses, and hidden caches.

Spent at:
- **Munchkin tinkers** — shoe upgrades.
- **Winkie smiths** — Tin Man upgrades.
- **Gillikin weavers** — companion meter capacity.
- **Glinda's shrine** — basket capacity, late-game conveniences.

No premium currency. No microtransactions.

## Upgrade Tracks

| Track | Tiers | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shoes | 4 | +charge, longer teleport, decoy afterimage, chain blink |
| Basket | 3 | +4 slots each |
| Companion meter | 3 each | Larger pool, faster out-of-combat refill |
| Companion kit | 2 each | One meaningful upgrade per ability |
| Health | 2 | +1 heart container (boss rewards only) |

## Boss Rewards

Bosses grant **verbs**, not numbers:

| Boss | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Witch of the East | The Shoes |
| Rogue Munchkin Marshal *(proposed)* | Scarecrow |
| Mombi | Lion + Ozma's blessing (meter capacity) |
| Witch of the West | Tin Man's final upgrade / hazard immunity |
| The Wizard | Ending choice |

## Difficulty Curve

- Enemy **count and composition** scale, not HP sponges.
- New enemy families force new kit answers.
- Optional challenge shrines grant cosmetics, not power.

Optional: keep all kits, remix enemy waves, add “Wicked” modifiers. **Cut first if schedule slips.**
