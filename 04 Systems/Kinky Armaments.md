# Kinky Armaments

Beyond standard attacks, Dorothy and her companions can utilize "Tools of Allure"—weapons and trinkets that manipulate the primal desires of their victims.

## Weaponry: The Tools of Control

These weapons prioritize manipulation, binding, and submission over raw damage.

| Weapon | Effect | Gameplay Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Lace Whip** | Binding / Pull | Deals low damage but "binds" the target, pulling them toward Dorothy for a follow-up Kick. |
| **Silk Bindings** | Immobilization | Projectiles that cocoon an enemy in silk, rendering them immobile and highly susceptible to Allure. |
| **Velvet Blindfold** | Sensory Deprivation | A throwable item that blinds an enemy, increasing the duration of "Enamored" states. |
| **The Gilded Chain** | Tethering | Tethers two enemies together; damage dealt to one is shared with the other. |

## Trinkets: The Auras of Desire

Trinkets are passive or activated items that alter the environment's reaction to the player.

### 1. Hypnotic Perfume (Consumable/Activated)
An aura that fills the immediate area. Enemies caught in the scent enter an **"Enamored"** state:
- They stop attacking Dorothy.
- They may actively protect her from other enemies for a short duration.
- They are stunned if Dorothy performs a "Blink" nearby.

### 2. Scent of Submission (Passive)
A subtle pheromonal aura that alters enemy AI:
- **Passive Effect**: Enemies are less aggressive and will not attack Dorothy first.
- **Trade-off**: Once an enemy is triggered to attack, their speed is increased as they are driven by a primal need to reach her.

### 3. The Mirror Shard (Active)
Reflects the "Perfect" face of the victim back at them.
- **Effect**: Causes the enemy to freeze in a state of self-infatuation, leaving them open to a critical hit.

## Integration with Core Loop
These armaments transform the combat loop from "Fight $\rightarrow$ Kill" to "Manipulate $\rightarrow$ Bind $\rightarrow$ Execute."
