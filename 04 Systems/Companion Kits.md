# Companion Kits

Full ability definitions for the three summons. Meters are thematic and exclusive to each companion.

## Scarecrow — Brain

| Ability | Input | Cost | Cooldown | Effect | Upgrade |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Murder of Crows** | A1 | 25 | 8 s | Sends a crow swarm to a target area; repeated pecks, interrupts casts | Wider area, more crows |
| **Confusing Caw** | A2 | 30 | 12 s | Enemies in radius attack nearest ally / wander for 4 s | Longer duration, jumps to 2 targets |
| **Straw Decoy** | A3 | 20 | 10 s | Drops a double that pulls aggro for 5 s; explodes into straw | Decoy taunts bosses briefly |
| **Field Stitch** | A4 (ult) | 100 | 25 s | Tethers up to 4 enemies; damage is shared between them | Tethers also slow |

**Identity:** crowd control, setup, misdirection. Weak vs. single armored targets.

## Cross-Kit Rules

- Only one companion active; kit swaps are the strategy layer.
- Ultimates require a full meter and a 1 s cast — vulnerable by design.
- No ability should one-shot an elite; they create openings, not endings.
- Every kit must have a traversal use (Crows trigger switches, Axe cuts barriers, Roar scatters blockers).
