## The One-Page Pitch

- **Genre:** 3D action-adventure with summon-based combat and light stealth.
- **Camera:** Third-person action camera, readability-first arenas.
- **Length target:** 8–12 hours, premium single-player.
- **Platform:** PC/Steam Deck first; consoles later.
- **Fantasy:** *You are small and alone. You borrow power from broken friends. You go home.*
- **Hook:** Summon one companion at a time; each has its own resource meter (Courage / Heart / Brain) and a kit built around a single fantasy. Dorothy's silver shoes are the mobility verb that ties every fight together.
- **Structure:** Black-and-white Kansas prologue → house interior → steerable flight → landing on the East Witch → four regions, four bosses, three companions, the Wizard.
