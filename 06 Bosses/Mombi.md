# Mombi

> **Founder beat:** “Commissioned by the Wizard to keep Princess Ozma from ruling Oz. King Kong / Frankenstein / zombie.”

## Fantasy

A **hulking, stitched-together enforcer** — part siege monster, part corpse, part lightning rod. She is the Wizard's hand in the wilds, and she keeps the true ruler of Oz locked in a boy's body.

## Silhouette & Read

- Towering (3–4× Dorothy), asymmetric: one massive arm, one withered arm, stitched torso.
- Lightning coils along her stitches; a cage-lantern on her back holds “Tip.”
- Moves like a marionette with too-tight strings; lurches, then explodes into speed.

## Arena

**Mombi's manor** in the Wilds: a collapsing dollhouse of a building with a central courtyard, breakable walls, and a lightning rod. Phase 3 opens the roof to a storm.

## Phases

| Phase | Question | Mechanics | Counter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P1 · The March** | Can you dodge a giant? | Slow telegraphed sweeps, debris throws, ground slam | Blink through, kick debris back |
| **P2 · The Storm** | Can you control the room? | Lightning strikes, stitched adds crawl from walls, cage-lantern pulses | Lion Roar interrupts; Scarecrow Crows on adds |
| **P3 · The Unstitching** | Can you break armor fast? | Armor plates, grab attempts, storm intensifies | Tin Man Axe severs stitches; Gilded Edge stuns |

## The Key Mechanic — Stitches

- Glowing stitched seams are **weak points**.
- Hitting a seam with the axe (or a kicked debris) **severs** it; three severances trigger a stagger.
- Severing **removes an ability** from Mombi for the rest of the fight (e.g., no more lightning).
- This turns the fight into a **target-prioritization puzzle**, not a DPS race.

## Lion's Role

- **Roar** interrupts her storm-cast and scatters stitched adds.
- **Coward's Cloak** lets Dorothy reposition behind her for seam hits.
- If the player ignores the Lion, P2 becomes chaos.

## The Ozma Reveal

At 25% health, the cage-lantern shatters and **Tip** falls free — revealing Ozma. Mombi panics; the fight becomes a **protect-Ozma** phase. Ozma's light weakens Mombi's stitches.

## Rewards

- **Lion joins.**
- Ozma freed → endgame legitimacy.
- Shoe Tier 2 upgrade materials.

## Voice

- “The Wizard gave me a kingdom to keep. I keep it *quiet*.”
- “Little girl. You are not even a rumor yet.”
- On defeat: “He said… I would never have to be small again…”
