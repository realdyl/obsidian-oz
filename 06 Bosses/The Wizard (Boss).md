# The Wizard — Final Boss

> The fight is not against a man. It is against **the machine he built** — and the lie a whole land agreed to believe.

## Structure

| Phase | Fantasy | Mechanics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P1 · Spectacle** | The “great and powerful” Oz | Giant projected head, illusion clones, stage traps, guard waves |
| **P2 · The Machine** | The wizard's real power | Mechanical constructs, thumper pistons, curtain mechanisms, shifting throne room |
| **P3 · The Man** | The truth | No boss bar; a chase/choice through a collapsing backstage |

## P1 — Spectacle

- Enormous floating head projection; voice shakes the room.
- Illusion clones of Dorothy's companions (twisted versions) attack.
- Environmental stage hazards: trapdoors, spotlight burn, pyrotechnics.
- **Counter:** the companions call out the fakes (Scarecrow spots logic errors, Lion smells fear, Tin Man hears gears).
- Ends when Dorothy **blinks through the projection** and finds the curtain.

## P2 — The Machine

- The throne room reconfigures into a factory.
- Constructs shaped like the Wizard's propaganda (iron guards, mechanical heads).
- Arena hazards tied to the city's infrastructure (steam, gears, glass).
- **Counter:** use every kit; the machine has seams for the axe, crowds for the crows, gaps for the Lion.
- Ends when the machine's core is exposed.

## P3 — The Man

- No health bar. A short, tense sequence behind the curtain.
- The Wizard flees through collapsing scaffolding; Dorothy pursues with the Shoes.
- Final interaction: **a choice, not a kill.**
  - **Go home now** — the Shoes open the road; Oz is left to Ozma.
  - **Stay and rebuild** — Dorothy gives the Shoes to Ozma; ambiguous, warmer ending.
  - **Take the machine** — secret/“Wicked” ending; Dorothy becomes the next Wizard. (Optional, unlockable.)

## Theme

The final boss is **the story you tell to avoid being small**. Dorothy wins by refusing to believe it — and by letting her friends be the proof.

## Rewards

- Ending cinematic.
- New Game+ unlock (optional).
- Gallery: “The Man Behind the Curtain.”

## Voice

- (Broadcast) “I AM OZ. I HAVE ALWAYS BEEN OZ.”
- (Behind curtain) “Do you know how hard it is to keep a lie this big standing?”
- (Final) “If I'm not a wizard… what am I supposed to be?”
