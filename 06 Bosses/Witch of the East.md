# Witch of the East — Cold Open

> **Founder beat:** “You land on her.”

## Fantasy

The game opens in black-and-white Kansas, puts the player inside the house as the tornado takes it, and lets them **steer the house onto a tyrant**. The “boss” is the **aftermath**: her dying magic, her legacy, and the shoes.

## Structure (12–16 minutes)

| Beat | What happens | Mode | Teaches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 · Kansas | Farm, storm, Dorothy runs for the house | Cutscene, B&W | Tone |
| 1 · The Canvas | Inside the house on a canvas; dodge debris, close the window | Playable, B&W | Move, interact |
| 2 · Flight | House mid-air; steer past obstacles; aim at the Witch | Playable, B&W | Steering, aim |
| 3 · Impact | House lands on the Witch; color floods in | Playable→cut | Payoff |
| 4 · Curse | Dying magic animates debris and munchkin puppets | Playable | Blink dodge |
| 5 · Legs | Enchanted legs/shoes pull Dorothy in | Playable | Kick |
| 6 · Shoes | The Shoes choose Dorothy; the curse breaks | Cutscene | Reward |

## Design Notes

- **Black and white until impact.** Color is the reward for landing the house; it is the game's first big visual beat.
- **The Canvas is the tutorial room.** A literal canvas on the floor gives Dorothy a clean, readable surface and a reason to move around the room; closing the window is the first interact.
- **Flight is player-aimed, not on-rails.** A soft reticle makes the Witch the obvious target. Missing still lands in Munchkinland (story holds); only the perfect-landing cosmetic is lost.
- **Not a full boss bar.** A cinematic encounter with interactive beats.
- The “enemy” is the **curse**, not the corpse. Keep it eerie, not gory.
- First control lesson: move → interact → steer → blink → kick, in that order.
- No companion yet; this is the baseline verb test.
- The Munchkins watch from the edges, terrified — seed the Lollipop Guild.

## Failure State

The prologue cannot be failed out of the story. Debris resets Dorothy to the last safe spot; a missed landing still reaches Munchkinland. The curse cannot kill permanently; it resets the beat. This is a tutorial, not a gate.

## Rewards

- **The Shoes** (blink + short teleport).
- First look at the regime's damage (the East witch's debt ledger, propaganda posters).
- Quest hook: the Lollipop Guild needs help with collaborators.

## Music

Kansas: wind, a distant hymn, no score. The house interior: creaking wood and rising wind. Flight: a single held string that climbs. Impact: silence for one beat, then color returns with the first full chord; the Shoes' chime ends the sequence.
