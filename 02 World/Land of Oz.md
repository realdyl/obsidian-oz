# The Land of Oz

## Premise

Oz is a storybook continent held together by **belief**. It used to run on wonder. Now it runs on **fear and machinery**: the Wizard's Emerald City broadcasts order, Mombi enforces it in the wilds, and the Wicked Witch of the West controls the air. The land is still beautiful — but the beauty is a coat of paint over something starving.

Dorothy's arrival is an accident that becomes a threat: she is the first person in decades who does not believe the Wizard is a god.

## Geography (Book Canon)

Oz is divided into four quadrants around the Emerald City, each with a ruling witch in Baum's books:

| Quadrant | Country | Ruler (book) | Game role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| East | Munchkinland | Wicked Witch of the East (dead) | Act 1 — tutorial, Lollipop Guild, Scarecrow |
| North | Gillikin Country | Locasta, Good Witch of the North | Act 2 — Ozma, Mombi, Lion |
| West | Winkie Country | Wicked Witch of the West | Act 3 — Tin Man, flying monkeys |
| South | Quadling Country | Glinda, Good Witch of the South | Act 4 — endgame guidance |
| Center | Emerald City | The Wizard | Finale |

> [!note] Film vs. book
> The 1939 film moves Glinda to the North. We follow the **books**: Locasta rules the North, Glinda the South.

## The Machine of Oz

- **The Wizard** is a fraud who built a regime on spectacle: projection, mechanical heads, paid-off guards.
- **Mombi** is his enforcer, keeping the rightful ruler (Ozma/Tip) hidden and the wilds afraid.
- **The Wicked Witch of the West** is a rival power he cannot control — his “monster” to point at.
- **The Wicked Witch of the East** ran Munchkinland through debt and fear until the house landed.

## Magic Rules

1. **Belief has weight.** Places that are believed in stay alive; abandoned places rot into “static” (visual noise, dead color).
2. **Witches are bound by their domains.** Their power weakens outside their country.
3. **The Shoes are older than the witches.** They are a key, not a weapon — long-distance travel, and eventually home.
4. **No resurrection.** Dead witches stay dead; their magic lingers as curses and constructs.
5. **Ozma is the land's true key.** Her return destabilizes the Wizard's machine.

## Factions

| Faction | Want | Relationship to Dorothy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Munchkins (Lollipop Guild) | Freedom from rogue collaborators | First allies; give Scarecrow lead |
| Rogue Munchkins | Power under the East witch's legacy | Enemies in Act 1 |
| Gillikins | Protection from Mombi | Locasta's people; quest givers |
| Winkies | Survival under the West witch | Oppressed; Tin Man's people |
| Flying Monkeys | Bound by a curse to the West witch | Enemies, then potential allies |
| Emerald City Guard | Maintain order | Obstacle, then turn |

## Visual Identity

- **Storybook surrealism:** painted backdrops, exaggerated silhouettes, practical-feeling props.
- **Corruption language:** candy colors desaturate near regime control; gold filigree hides rust; the Emerald City is green glass over scaffolding.
- **Scale:** Oz is built for giants. Doors, stairs, and furniture make Dorothy tiny.
