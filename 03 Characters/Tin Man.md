# Tin Man (Tin Woodman)

**Role:** Third companion. Armor-breaker, tank, terrain tool.
**Resource:** **Heart** (thematic meter).
**Found:** Rusted solid in the Winkie woods, still standing guard over nothing ([[Main Questline]] Act 3).

> [!note] Open design
> The founder notes don't specify where the Tin Man is found. Proposed: **Winkie Country**, as the West witch's discarded “perfect soldier.” This ties his heart arc to the regime that built him. See [[Open Questions]].

## Personality

Formal, gentle, mortified by his own strength. He apologizes before he swings. He believes he has no heart; every act of restraint proves otherwise. He is the emotional anchor of the group.

## Combat Identity

The **wall and the key**: holds space, breaks armor, and reshapes the environment. Slow, deliberate, devastating when timed. Weak to mobility-heavy enemies (that's the shoes/Lion's job).

## Kit (summary — full table in [[Companion Kits]])

| Ability | Fantasy |
| --- | --- |
| **Hacking Axe** | Heavy cleave; breaks armor/shields |
| **Oil Slick** | Slippery zone; enemies slide/stumble |
| **Gilded Edge** *(the “gold ability”)* | Charged axe turns a target to gold — hard stun, shatter combo |
| **Riveted Guard** | Plant as a shield wall; Dorothy can blink off him |

## Unlock Arc

1. Winkie Country is a machine graveyard run by the West witch.
2. The Tin Man is chained as a “failed prototype.”
3. Dorothy must find the **oil can** (a mini-quest through the rust works).
4. Freeing him unlocks the axe (environmental key) and his kit.

## Boss Test

The Winkie **Ironclad** mini-boss *(proposed)* is armored and immobile; only the axe and oil can break it. Teaches: *armor is a puzzle, not a health bar.*

## Voice & Barks

- “I am so sorry about this.” *(swings)*
- “If I had a heart, I think it would be pounding.”
- On victory: “We did that together. That feels… warm.”
