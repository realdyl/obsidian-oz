# Cowardly Lion

**Role:** Second companion. Fear, stealth, disruption.
**Resource:** **Courage** (thematic meter).
**Found:** Among the **lions, tigers, and bears** of the Wilds, hiding from everything including himself ([[Q3 - Locasta & the Princess]]).

## Personality

Loud when safe, silent when scared. A bully to his own reflection. His bluster is armor over terror, and his arc is discovering that courage is *acting while afraid* — which he does every time Dorothy needs him.

## Combat Identity

The **escape artist**. He creates space, breaks morale, and enables stealth. He is the answer to overwhelming odds and the key to Mombi's arena.

> [!important] Summon Restriction (founder note)
> **The Lion cannot be summoned close to enemies.** He must be called from a safe distance or from stealth. This is a design constraint, not a bug: it forces planning and makes his entrance a dramatic beat.

## Kit (summary — full table in [[Companion Kits]])

| Ability | Fantasy |
| --- | --- |
| **Lion's Roar** | AoE fear — enemies flee, morale breaks |
| **Coward's Cloak** | Stealth field; Dorothy becomes hidden |
| **Pounce** | Gap-closer/opener from stealth |
| **Tail Sweep** | Knockback to create distance |

## Unlock Arc

1. Locasta needs the path cleared and Ozma freed from Mombi.
2. The Wilds are ruled by fear; the Lion is the most frightened thing in them.
3. Dorothy calms him (no fight — a **trust encounter**), and he joins.
4. Mombi is the boss that tests him (roar interrupts her attacks).

## Boss Test

**Mombi** ([[Mombi]]): her telegraphs are interrupted by Roar; her adds are scattered by fear. If the player never uses the Lion's kit, the fight is a slog.

## Voice & Barks

- “I'm not scared. I'm… strategically alarmed.”
- “RUN. I mean—bravely retreat!”
- On victory: “Did you see that? I did that. I think. Don't tell anyone I cried.”
