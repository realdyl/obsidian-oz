# The Wizard

**Role:** Primary antagonist and final boss.
**Truth:** A fraud. A showman with machinery, projection, and hired muscle.

## The Regime

The Wizard does not rule by magic; he rules by **spectacle and scarcity**.

- The Emerald City is a stage set: green glass, hidden scaffolding, projection towers.
- He commissions **Mombi** to keep Ozma hidden and the wilds terrified.
- He lets the **West witch** run rampant because a monster makes a savior necessary.
- He controls information: maps are wrong, history is edited, the “great and powerful” myth is broadcast.

## Motive

He is not evil for evil's sake. He is a small man who found a big machine and cannot afford to be seen. Ozma's return would expose him; Dorothy's arrival accelerates the collapse he has been delaying for years.

## Presence in the Game

| Act | Role |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Distant myth; propaganda broadcasts; guards repeat his slogans |

## The Reveal

The “great head” is a projection. The “voice” is a machine. The “wizard” is a frightened man behind a curtain — and the fight is against **the machine he built**, not the man.

**Theme:** the most dangerous lies are the ones a whole land agrees to believe.

## Boss Fight

See [[The Wizard (Boss)]]. Three phases: **Spectacle → Machine → Man**, ending in a choice, not a kill.

## Voice & Barks

- (Broadcast) “THE GREAT AND POWERFUL OZ SEES ALL.”
- (Behind the curtain) “You're ruining the timing. Do you know how long I've kept this running?”
- (Final) “If I'm not a wizard… what am I supposed to be?”
