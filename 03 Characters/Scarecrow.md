# Scarecrow

**Role:** First companion. Crowd control, confusion, misdirection.
**Resource:** **Brain** (thematic meter).
**Found:** Impaled on a pole in the fields outside the Lollipop Guild's territory ([[Q2 - The Lollipop Guild]]).

## Personality

Talkative, self-deprecating, secretly the sharpest person in any room. He believes he has no brain; the player watches him out-think everyone. His humor is a defense mechanism, and his arc is learning that intelligence isn't a prize you're given — it's what you do.

## Combat Identity

The **problem-solver**: he doesn't out-damage enemies, he *rearranges* them. Best against crowds and ambush setups. Weak against armored single targets (that's Tin Man's job).

## Kit (summary — full table in [[Companion Kits]])

| Ability | Fantasy |
| --- | --- |
| **Murder of Crows** | Send a swarm to harass/peck an area |
| **Confusing Caw** | Enemies attack each other / wander |
| **Straw Decoy** | Drop a double that pulls aggro |
| **Field Stitch** | Tether two enemies together briefly |

## Unlock Arc

1. Locasta asks Dorothy to clear the road and deal with Mombi.
2. On the way, rogue munchkins have strung up the Scarecrow as a warning.
3. Freeing him triggers the **Rogue Munchkin Marshal** boss *(proposed — see [[Boss Framework]]).*
4. Beating it earns the Scarecrow's trust and his kit.

## Boss Test

The Marshal fight is designed so that **crowd control is mandatory** — adds overwhelm the player unless they use Crows/Confuse. Teaches: *choose the friend, not the damage.*

## Voice & Barks

- “I don't have a brain, but I do have opinions.”
- “That's a lot of angry little men. Statistically, we should leave.”
- On victory: “See? No brain required. …That was a joke. Mostly.”
