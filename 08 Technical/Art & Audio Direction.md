# Art & Audio Direction

## Visual Pillars

1. **Real stylized 3D, not voxel/block.** Ship art is continuous meshes, light, scale, and silhouette. Prototype CSG/capsules are scaffold only — **never** accept a Minecraft-adjacent product look ([[GUI & UX Roadmap]] anti-targets).
2. **Storybook-stylized, not photoreal mandate.** Hand-painted or stylized PBR textures, readable proportions, visible craft — premium third-person adventure, not a documentary sim.
3. **Oz is beautiful and wrong.** Saturated wonder undercut by rot, propaganda, and stitching.
4. **Silhouette first.** Every character reads at 20 m.
5. **Color = faction and mood.** Munchkinland candy-bright; Gillikin cool greens; Wilds amber/rust; Winkie iron-grey; Emerald City poisoned green.
6. **The Duality of the Dream.** Every environment and character should have a "perfect" face and a "broken" side. Contrast the whimsical with the morbid.
7. **Horrific and Aesthetic.** Borrow the "Beautiful Corruption" of *Alice: Madness Returns* / *Alice: Asylum* mood (`IDEA.md`). High fashion and ornate beauty meeting visceral, unsettling decay — fully **authored 3D stages**, not greybox.

## Aesthetic Guidelines: Hedonistic Horror

### 1. Contrast & Saturation
- **Opposing Palettes**: Pair high-saturation "candy" colors (neon pinks, electric blues) with oppressive, deep shadows. 
- **Luminous Accents**: Use glowing, ethereal light sources (neon blues, toxic greens) to highlight points of interest in dark, decaying spaces.
- **The "Rot" Layer**: Beauty is the skin; rot is the bone. High-fashion aesthetics (latex, lace, silk) transitioning into visceral decay.

### 2. Surreal Architecture
- **Distorted Geometry**: Buildings should feel sentient or unstable. Lean into "wrong" angles, floating fragments, and impossible connections (e.g., a house held together by glowing wires or stitches).
- **Scale Manipulation**: Constant reminders of Dorothy's smallness. Huge, oppressive structures juxtaposed with tiny, fragile details.
- **Environmental Storytelling**: Landscapes that tell a story of a "broken machine." Ruined monuments to the Wizard's lies, overgrown by surreal flora.

### 3. Character Design (The "Asylum" Vibe)
- **Stylized Morbidity**: Characters should be charming but unnerving. Think "porcelain doll with a crack in the face."
- **Costume Narrative**: Clothing should reflect the character's internal struggle (e.g., the "Denial Dress" concept: perfect front, horrific back).
- **Theatricality**: Poses and animations should be slightly exaggerated, like a puppet or a stage play.
5. **The Duality of the Dream.** Every environment and character should have a "perfect" face and a "broken" side. Contrast the whimsical with the morbid (e.g., a beautiful dress with a rotting underside).

## Character Read

### Protagonist: Badass Dorothy
Dorothy is not a damsel; she is the most dangerous thing in the room.
- **The "Sexy" Factor**: Not sexualized, but "sexy" in her absolute competence and intensity. 
- **Vibe**: Fearless, focused, and relentless. Her movements should be precise and powerful.
- **Visual Contrast**: A small, innocent gingham aesthetic paired with a lethal, calculated combat style.

| Character | Silhouette |

| Scarecrow | Lanky, floppy, wide hat; moves like a puppet |

| Lion | Big, hunched, mane like a storm cloud; tail always nervous |
| Mombi | Asymmetric giant; one arm too big; stitched seams glow |

## UI

- Storybook frame motifs; paper texture, hand-drawn icons — **on top of** a real 3D world, not instead of one.
- Basket UI is a literal basket close-up.
- Health as a stitched heart; meters as Brain/Heart/Courage icons.
- No sci-fi HUD. No damage numbers by default (optional).
- Prefer **minimal permanent chrome** (Rockstar-class craft: world and camera carry identity; prompts appear in context) — still Oz paper language, not GTA radar clone.
- **Full polish + presentation roadmap / agent handoff:** [[GUI & UX Roadmap]] (G chrome + P world tracks, Alice + Rockstar research rules, anti-Minecraft bar).

## Animation Style

- Snappy, readable, slightly theatrical.
- Anticipation frames are long; recovery is short.
- Companions have distinct idle personalities (Scarecrow fidgets, Tin Man oils himself, Lion hides behind Dorothy).

## Audio Pillars

1. **Folk instruments, wrong tuning.** Music box, fiddle, hurdy-gurdy, choir, low brass.
2. **Silence is a weapon.** The East witch's curse cuts all music to a drone.
3. **Diegetic first.** Wind, crows, oil can rattles, tin joints.
4. **Each companion has a motif** that layers in when summoned.

## Prologue Color Script

### The Opening Scene: Kansas
**Atmosphere**: A dusty, oppressive wasteland. A singular, lonely farmhouse surrounded by beaten-down, rotting fences. The environment should feel exhausted and drained of life.
- **Visuals**: High-contrast black and white. Soft grain. The dust should feel tangible, like a veil over the world.

1. **Kansas and the house interior:** pure black and white. High contrast, soft grain, no color grading tricks beyond luminance.
2. **Flight:** black and white with a single warm accent (the window light, Dorothy's shoes catching the storm).
3. **Impact:** one beat of white-out, then **color blooms outward from the landing point** — first the shoes, then the grass, then the Munchkins' faces.
4. **Rule:** no color anywhere before impact. The palette itself is the reward.

> [!note] Legal
> Black-and-white-to-color is a general cinematic technique. Do not reproduce specific 1939 film frames, costumes, or the sepia tone as trade dress; see [[IP & Legal]].

## SFX Priorities

- Window close: latch snap + wind drop.
- House flight: wood groan, rope snap, wind roar layered by speed.
- Landing: sub-bass impact + shattering glass + sudden quiet.
- Blink: air-tear whoosh + heel click.
- Kick: thump + cloth.
- Crow: wing burst.
- Axe: metal ring + wood chunk.
- Roar: layered big cat + room reverb.
- Oil: gurgle + slip.
