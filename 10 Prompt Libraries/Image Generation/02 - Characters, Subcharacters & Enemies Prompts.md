# 02 — Characters, Subcharacters & Enemies Prompt Library

Generate consistent **character turnarounds (`3:4` full-body on neutral dark `#160B1E` backdrop)** and **Grimoire portrait busts (`1:1`)** for every playable hero, NPC, animal, enemy, and boss in *Oz: The Road Home*.

---

## 1. Playable Party (Main Cast)

- **Dorothy Gale (Kansas Monochrome)**: `Full-body character sheet of Dorothy Gale, poised 20-year-old prairie girl in 1900s monochrome grey-and-white gingham pinafore dress, dark braided pigtails, scuffed farm boots, carrying a woven wicker basket, somber gothic Americana lighting, neutral dark grey backdrop`
- **Dorothy Gale (Oz Silver Shoes & Couture)**: `Full-body character sheet of Dorothy Gale in Oz, crisp royal-blue and white gingham Victorian bodice with flared skirt, gleaming enchanted Silver Shoes with buckled heels, determined gaze, dark-storybook fantasy style, neutral dark plum backdrop`
- **Dorothy Gale (Velvet Corset Couture)**: `Full-body character design of Dorothy Gale wearing opulent crimson-obsidian velvet corset gown with gold cross-lacing, scalloped black lace hem, silver shoes, holding a coiled crimson silk whip, dark gothic couture`
- **Scarecrow**: `Full-body character sheet of the Scarecrow, agile lean figure in patched ochre burlap tunic, straw tufts at wrists and collar, floppy pointed felt hat, clever mischievous posture, dark-storybook fantasy art`
- **Tin Woodman (Nick Chopper)**: `Full-body character sheet of the Tin Woodman, articulated riveted silver-tin plate armor, iconic inverted tin funnel cap, glowing crimson clockwork heart emblem on chest, clutching a heavy steel woodman's axe, gothic steampunk fantasy`
- **Cowardly Lion**: `Full-body character sheet of a majestic golden-tawny Lion standing proud yet slightly nervous, lush sculpted mane, expressive amber eyes, subtle gilded war-harness option, painterly dark fairy-tale style`
- **Toto (Cairn Terrier)**: `Character sheet of Toto, scruffy dark-brindle Cairn terrier with perky pointed ears, bright alert eyes, wearing a small silver-star collar, able to sit snugly inside a woven wicker basket, crisp stylized 3D character reference`

## 2. Subcharacters & Story NPCs

- **Aunt Em (Kansas)**: `Character concept of Aunt Em, weathered 1900s Kansas farm woman in her late 40s, hair in a neat bun, long grey wool skirt and stained work apron, stern yet deeply loving expression, monochrome sepia-grey palette`
- **Uncle Henry (Kansas)**: `Character concept of Uncle Henry, stoic early-50s prairie farmer in faded overalls, rolled sleeves, wide-brimmed dusty felt hat, weathered hands, monochrome sepia-grey palette`
- **Locasta — Good Witch of the North**: `Full-body concept of Locasta, ageless aristocratic sorceress in shimmering white-and-silver star-embroidered Victorian gown, high lace collar, carrying a delicate silver-tipped wand, serene porcelain-pale beauty, northern starlight halo`
- **Glinda — Good Witch of the South**: `Full-body concept of Glinda, radiant auburn-haired queen in flowing ruby-rose silk gown with hammered gold filigree crown and rose-quartz scepter, warm celestial glow`
- **Oscar Diggs — The Humbug Wizard**: `Character concept of Oscar Diggs, nervous 1900s Omaha carnival balloonist in faded pinstripe tailcoat, silk top hat, round spectacles, clutching a brass megaphone and lever box behind a crimson velvet curtain`
- **Queen of the Field Mice & Mice Team**: `Stylized dark-storybook concept of tiny intelligent brown-and-white field mice wearing miniature red-velvet harness straps pulling a wooden cart across a poppy field`
- **Kansas Prairie Chickens**: `Stylized 3D character reference of plump speckled grey-and-white Kansas farmyard hens and a strutting rooster pecking on straw, monochrome sepia-grey palette`
