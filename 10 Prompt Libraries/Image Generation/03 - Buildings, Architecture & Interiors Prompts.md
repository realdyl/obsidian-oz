# 03 — Buildings, Architecture & Interiors Prompt Library

Style references for every exterior building, landmark, gate, and enterable room across Kansas and Oz.

---

## 1. Kansas Farmstead Buildings (Monochrome Sepia-Grey)

- **Gale Farmhouse (Exterior)**: `1900s weathered clapboard Kansas prairie farmhouse with steep gabled shingle roof, covered front porch with wooden railings, window flower boxes, and one loose banging wooden shutter, monochrome sepia-grey palette`
- **Gale Farmhouse (Interior)**: `Cozy yet sparse 1900s prairie farmhouse parlor interior, worn wooden floorboards, braided rug, oak dining table with oil lamp, gingham curtains, ornate wooden treasure chest in the corner, warm monochrome sepia lighting`
- **Kansas Timber Barn (Exterior & Interior)**: `Tall weathered grey timber prairie barn with double hayloft doors, gambrel roof, and crisp "BARN" crest sign; interior filled with stacked hay bales, hanging harness leather, lantern light, and a hidden wooden ledger`
- **Timber Chicken Coop & Swinging Gate**: `Distinct small timber-slat raised chicken coop on stilt legs with sloped shed roof, wooden chicken ramp, wire-mesh fenced run with a hinged swinging wooden gate, and speckled hens inside, monochrome Kansas farm`
- **Lattice Windmill & Clothesline**: `Tall skeletal wooden-and-iron Kansas prairie windmill with spinning slatted rotor wheel beside a taut clothesline of billowing white sheets and aprons in rising storm wind`
- **Storm Cellar**: `Slanted heavy wooden storm cellar bulkhead doors set into a grassy mound beside the farmhouse with iron padlock and lantern glow`

## 2. Munchkinland Architecture (Surreal Pastel Storybook)

- **Munchkinland Cottage (Exterior)**: `Whimsical yet uncanny pastel-mint and rose-pink Munchkin storybook cottage with steep curved shingle roof, circular stained-glass windows, stone chimney, and gilded crest sign, dark fairy-tale lighting`
- **Munchkinland Cottage (Interior)**: `Lavish eccentric Munchkin parlor interior, checkered pastel tile floor, velvet settee, brass lantern chandelier, porcelain masks on the wall, and a gilded Desire Chest`
- **Crashed Kansas Farmhouse Wreckage**: `Grey monochrome wooden Kansas farmhouse lying crookedly crashed onto pastel Munchkinland grass beside the Yellow Brick Road, silver-gleaming shoes sticking out beneath the timber beam`
- **Locasta's Northern Shrine & Signposts**: `Carved timber Oz directional signpost with gilded arrow plaques beside a glowing silver-white starlight lantern post`

## 3. Winkie Country & Emerald City Landmarks

- **Winkie Iron Castle Courtyard**: `Oppressive gothic forged-iron fortress courtyard with riveted dark-iron battlements, tall arrow-slit towers, spiked iron portcullis gate, and a central stone-and-iron Water Cistern`
- **Emerald City Castle Ramparts (Disney-World Scale)**: `Breathtaking monumental emerald-green art-deco castle wall and grand gatehouse with 6 towering faceted crystal turrets, stepped cascading emerald fountains, golden arched double doors, and manicured topiary courtyard`
- **The Tailor of Vices Boutique**: `Opulent dark-plum and emerald Victorian dressmaker storefront on the Emerald Boulevard with velvet-draped display windows, brass mannequins, and a glowing gilded scissor crest sign`
- **Wizard's Velvet Curtain Throne Room**: `Dramatic dark-emerald marble throne chamber featuring a towering crimson velvet theater curtain hung from gilded brass rings, a central brass projection dais, and a hidden gear-work alcove behind the drape`
