# Character Voice Profiles & Dialogue Scripts (`Oz: The Road Home`)

> [!TIP]
> Generate voices in **ElevenLabs** (Voice Design or Library) or locally with **Kokoro-82M / Chatterbox TTS**. Export mono `.ogg` / `.wav` normalized to `-16 LUFS` into `oz/assets/audio/voices/<character>/<line_id>.ogg`.

## 1. Dorothy Gale (`voices/dorothy/`)
- **Voice Design Prompt**: `Young American woman, early 20s, warm subtle Midwestern Kansas cadence, clear articulate alto, grounded and fiercely composed with a dry edge under pressure — never squeaky or childish.`
- **ElevenLabs Settings**: Stability `48%`, Similarity `78%`, Style `32%`
- **Key Lines**:
  - `dorothy_basket.ogg`: *"There — Toto's basket. Now where's that dog?"*
  - `dorothy_shoes.ogg`: *"Silver. Cold as a prairie frost — and they fit like they knew I was coming."*
  - `dorothy_kick_01.ogg`: *"Stay back!"*
  - `dorothy_kick_02.ogg`: *"Out of my road!"*
  - `dorothy_whip_01.ogg`: *"Come closer."*
  - `dorothy_rapture.ogg`: *"Enough games — let's finish this."*
  - `dorothy_home.ogg`: *"Grey, dusty, and mine. We made it back, Toto."*

## 2. Aunt Em (`voices/aunt_em/`)

## 5. Scarecrow (`voices/scarecrow/`)

- **Voice Design Prompt**: `Sharp, imperious older woman, cold aristocratic rasp, venomous precision and bitter elegance — Baum book canon (pale, one-eyed, terrifyingly composed; NOT cartoonish cackling).`
