# *Oz: The Road Home* — Book-Length Runtime & Pacing Blueprint

> **North Star Mandate:** Finishing *Oz: The Road Home* from opening Kansas breeze to Kansas Homecoming should take roughly the same amount of time as **thoroughly reading L. Frank Baum's *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz* (1900) cover-to-cover**: **~3.5 to 4.5 hours (210–270 minutes)** for a thorough first playthrough.

---

## 1. The Baum (1900) Reading-Time Benchmark

- **Novel Length:** ~39,500 words across **24 chapters**.
- **Thorough Reading Pace:** 150–185 words per minute (savoring imagery, dialogue, and W.W. Denslow illustrations) = **3.5 to 4.5 hours**.
- **Design Translation:** Rather than inflating runtime with tedious grinding or empty hallways, every single one of Baum's 24 chapters is represented as a **playable spatial beat, environmental puzzle, multi-speaker conversation, or bespoke boss encounter** across our 7 Acts.

## 3. Five Anti-Filler Pillars (How to Keep 4 Hours Continuously Engaging)

1. **Interactive Back-and-Forth Conversations Everywhere (Zero Monologues)**
   - **Hard Dialogue Rule:** No single NPC or narrator may speak more than **2 lines in a row** without Dorothy—or an active companion (Scarecrow, Tin Man, Lion, Toto)—reacting, questioning, or bantering back.
   - Dynamic portrait medallions (`DialogueUI`) swap on every turn so conversations feel like a real 2026 RPG cast.
   - Add **optional roadside banter triggers** (e.g., Scarecrow & Tin Man debating whether a brain or a heart is better while walking down the Yellow Brick Road, straight from Baum Ch. 5!).

2. **True 5-Character Swapping Puzzles (`1`–`5` / `Tab` + `Z`/`X`/`C`)**
   - Every Act after L1 must contain at least **2 environmental puzzles** that require swapping Leads and using their `F` kit or `Z`/`X`/`C` Extra Superpowers:
     - **Dorothy:** Silver-Shoe Blink across chasms + Lace Whip grapple/switch pull + Perfume charm.
     - **Scarecrow:** Crow Storm to trigger high bell-switches + Straw Decoy to hold pressure plates + Lantern Spark to dispel dark fog.
     - **Tin Man:** Axe cleave on rusted chains/deadfalls + Oil-Slick speed ramps + Heart-Chime shield through hazard corridors.
     - **Lion:** Roar to shatter brittle stone barriers/frighten ambushes + Apex Pounce across wide ravines.
     - **Toto:** Golden Scent Trail to hidden paths + Prairie Dig for buried keys + small-gap crawlspaces & Curtain-Pull interrupts.

3. **Distinct Sub-Zones Within Each Act**
   - Instead of one straight 60m corridor, each major Act (L2–L6) expands into **3–4 connected scenic terraces** separated by natural landmarks (stone bridges, ravines, orchard gates, castle ramparts, market squares, porcelain stairs) so the player feels a true *odyssey* across Oz.

4. **Multi-Phase Story Bosses & Set-Piece Encounters**
   - Every major Act culminates in a memorable **2- or 3-phase encounter** with environmental mechanics (not just HP sponges):
     - L2: **Lollipop Guild Marshal** (shield break + rogue summon).
     - L3: **Kalidah Shadow-Chimera** + **Poppy Rescue Escort**.
     - L4: **Wicked Witch of the West** (3 phases: Wolf/Crow vanguard → Winged Monkey dive-bombs → Grounded Cistern Water Melt).
     - L5: **Humbug Wizard Illusion Gauntlet** (overload 2 brass projector pylons while dodging floating-head fire/plasma sweeps, then Toto pulls the curtain).
     - L6: **Giant Forest Spider** (Lion's crowning duel) + **Hammer-Head Ridge**.

5. **Persistent Progression Across All 4 Hours**
   - **Desire Fragments** earned from combat Style Ranks (`D → ★ SOVEREIGN`), Whimsies, Vile Requests, and Sanctums feed continuously into **The Tailor of Vices** (4 bespoke couture outfits per character + Armament upgrades + Curio Charms) so the player's build and wardrobe visibly evolve from Act 1 to Act 7.
