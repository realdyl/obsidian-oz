# *Oz: The Road Home* — Gemini 4 Carbon Master Plan (`P1.4 → P12.4`)

> **Goal:** Elevate *Oz: The Road Home* so **each of the 7 Acts takes ~15 minutes to 100% complete** through expanded scenic terraces, tactile environmental micro-activities, and **fully beautified `Z` / `X` / `C` extra superpowers with held 3D props & bespoke VFX for all 5 playable characters** (Dorothy, Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Cowardly Lion, and Toto).

---

## 1. The 15-Minute-Per-Act Pacing & Terrace Expansion Architecture

Rather than bloating maps with excessive collectible spam (current Whimsy density is already well-balanced), each Act achieves a **natural, rewarding ~15-minute runtime** by combining **wider/longer multi-route terraces** with **3–4 distinct interactive sub-activities** per level:

| Act | Target Runtime | Expanded Terrace Geometry | Signature Interactive Sub-Activities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L1 · Kansas Farmyard** | ~12–15 min | Expand farmyard south/west pasture terraces (`140×140m`) with creek culvert bridge, windbreak treeline, and storm cellar lane. | 1) Herd 3 panicked hens back through the 2-way swinging coop gate · 2) Inspect Storm Barometer & Barn Loft Heirloom · 3) Latch 3 rattling shutters · 4) Cyclone wind-gust sprint into the lifting farmhouse. |
| **L2 · Munchkinland** | ~15–20 min | Widen YBR spine into **4 stepped scenic terraces** (*Crash Plaza*, *Confectioner's Market Terrace*, *Cornfield Labyrinth*, *Timber Ravine*) connected by stone bridges & elevated overlooks. | 1) Free the Indebted Confectioner (3 Tax Coffers + Crow-Bell) · 2) Toto Scent Trail treasure dig · 3) **Velvet Sanctum of Echoes** timed trial · 4) **2-Phase Lollipop Guild Marshal** boss fight. |
| **L3 · Deadly Poppy Field** | ~15 min | Extend scarlet canyon (`160m` length) with raised wind-swept **Clear-Air Terraces** flanking the valley floor + elevated cliff bypasses. | 1) Wake 2 lost Field-Mouse Scouts on the side ridges · 2) Defeat the **Kalidah Shadow-Chimera** guarding the eastern canyon · 3) Hitch & escort the Mice Cart around sleeping hazards · 4) **Velvet Sanctum of Thorns**. |
| **L4 · Winkie Iron Castle** | ~15–20 min | Add climbable **Battlement Ramparts** & twin **Courtyard Cistern Terraces** overlooking the iron keep. | 1) Sunder 2 Winkie Slave-Shackle Pylons with Tin Man · 2) Dodge Winged-Monkey dive-bomb perches on the ramparts · 3) **Sanctum of the Winged Cap** · 4) **3-Phase Wicked Witch of the West** + Cistern melt finale. |
| **L5 · Emerald City** | ~15–18 min | Expand Grand Emerald Boulevard with **East & West Market Terraces**, fountain plazas (solid marble basins), and a walk-in **Tailor of Vices** Couture Salon. | 1) Toggle `[G]` Locked Green Spectacles to uncover 3 Humbug Illusion Seals · 2) Visit the physical Tailor of Vices salon · 3) **Velvet Sanctum of Clockwork** · 4) Overload 2 Palace Projector Pylons before the Curtain Reveal. |
| **L6 · Glinda's Quadling Court** | ~12–15 min | Expand Alabaster Gardens with a **China Country Porcelain Terrace** & twin stepped Reflecting Pool walkways. | 1) Attune 3 China Country Porcelain Chimes · 2) Mirror-light alignment puzzle across the reflecting pools · 3) Glinda's counsel & ceremonial 3-heel-click finale. |
| **L7 · Kansas Homecoming** | ~8–10 min | Mirror L1's expanded sepia prairie with interactive Porch Memory Album & Barn/Coop revisits. | 1) Revisit Aunt Em, Uncle Henry & Toto · 2) Browse unlocked Shattered Memories on the porch · 3) Unlock **`★ THE VELVET ROAD` (New Game+)**. |

## 3. Execution Sequence (`P1.4 → P12.4`)

1. **P1.4**: Physical held 3D ability props for all 5 playables + unified `Z`/`X`/`C` Extra Ability bar in `ArmamentDock` (auto-swaps icons when you press `1–5` / `Tab`!).
2. **P2.4**: Full VFX & animation beautification of Dorothy's 4 Armaments (ribbon trails, crystal atomizer mist, chain links, multi-segment physics whip).
3. **P3.4**: Scarecrow & Tin Woodman `Z`/`X`/`C` kits (held lantern, oiler, steam plumes, decoy, shockwave).
4. **P4.4**: Cowardly Lion & Toto `Z`/`X`/`C` kits (Apex Pounce arc, Golden Scent Ribbon to nearest quest/secret, Ankle-Nip).
5. **P5.4**: Global **Solid World Prop Audit** (verify every rock, fountain, lamppost, bench, cart, and tree has clean Layer-1 collision & zero walk-throughs).
6. **P6.4 → P11.4**: Expand terraces & 15-minute sub-activities sequentially across **L1 Kansas → L2 Munchkinland → L3 Poppy Canyon → L4 Winkie Ramparts → L5 Emerald Boulevard → L6 Glinda's Gardens & L7 Homecoming**.
7. **P12.4**: Full 5-suite headless QA + new `superpowers_p4_sim.gd`, Obsidian log sync, and signed git release.
