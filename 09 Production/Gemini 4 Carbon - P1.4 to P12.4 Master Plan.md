# *Oz: The Road Home* — Gemini 4 Carbon Master Plan (`P1.4 → P12.4`)

> **Goal:** Elevate *Oz: The Road Home* to a **full book-length odyssey (~3.5 to 4.5 hours total — matching the time to thoroughly read L. Frank Baum's 24-chapter 1900 novel cover-to-cover)**, with **interactive back-and-forth conversations** across every cast member and **fully beautified `Z` / `X` / `C` extra superpowers with held 3D props & bespoke VFX for all 5 playable characters** (Dorothy, Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, Cowardly Lion, and Toto).
>
> **Companion Pacing Doc:** See [[Book-Length Runtime & Pacing Blueprint]] for the complete 24-chapter-to-7-Act minute budget (`240–300 min` total) and anti-filler design pillars.

## 1. Book-Length Pacing (~3.5–4.5 Hours Total) & Multi-Terrace Expansion Architecture

Rather than bloating maps with collectible spam (current Whimsy density is already well-balanced), each Act achieves **authentic Baum 1900 book-length pacing** by mapping all **24 chapters** into **multi-terrace scenic zones**, **interactive back-and-forth party banter**, **5-character environmental puzzles**, and **signature set pieces**:

| Act | Baum Chapters | Target Runtime | Expanded Terrace Geometry | Signature Interactive Sub-Activities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **L1 · Kansas Farmyard** | Ch. 1 | **15–20 min** | Expand farmyard south/west pasture terraces (`140×140m`) with creek culvert bridge, windbreak treeline, and storm cellar lane. | 1) Herd 3 panicked hens back through the 2-way swinging coop gate · 2) Inspect Storm Barometer & Barn Loft Heirloom · 3) Interactive Aunt Em & Uncle Henry banter · 4) Cyclone wind-gust sprint into the lifting farmhouse. |
| **L2 · Munchkinland** | Ch. 2–6 | **45–55 min** | Widen YBR spine into **4 stepped scenic terraces** (*Crash Plaza*, *Confectioner's Market Terrace*, *Cornfield Labyrinth*, *Timber Ravine*) connected by stone bridges & elevated overlooks. | 1) Interactive Locasta & Munchkin Council · 2) Free the Indebted Confectioner (3 Tax Coffers + Crow-Bell) · 3) Toto Scent Trail treasure dig · 4) **Velvet Sanctum of Echoes** · 5) **2-Phase Lollipop Guild Marshal** boss fight. |
| **L3 · Kalidah Wilds & Poppy Field** | Ch. 7–9 | **40–50 min** | Add **Kalidah Chasm** (Tin Man timber bridge) & extend scarlet canyon (`160m`) with raised **Clear-Air Terraces** flanking the valley floor. | 1) Kalidah Chasm bridge & **Kalidah Shadow-Chimera** boss · 2) Wake 2 lost Field-Mouse Scouts on side ridges · 3) Crystal Wind-Fountain & Mice Cart rescue escort · 4) **Velvet Sanctum of Thorns**. |
| **L4 · Winkie Iron Castle** | Ch. 12–13 | **45–55 min** | Add scorched Winkie Plains approach, climbable **Battlement Ramparts**, Iron Kitchen/Forge interiors, & twin **Courtyard Cistern Terraces**. | 1) Survive Wolf/Crow/Bee vanguard waves · 2) Sunder 2 Winkie Slave-Shackle Pylons with Tin Man · 3) **Sanctum of the Winged Cap** · 4) **3-Phase Wicked Witch of the West** + Cistern melt finale. |

| **L7 · Kansas Homecoming** | Ch. 24 | **15–20 min** | Mirror L1's expanded sepia prairie with interactive Porch Memory Album & Barn/Coop revisits. | 1) Emotional back-and-forth reunion with Aunt Em, Uncle Henry & Toto · 2) Browse unlocked Shattered Memories on the porch · 3) Unlock **`★ THE VELVET ROAD` (New Game+)**. |
