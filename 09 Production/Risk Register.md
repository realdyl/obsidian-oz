# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |

| R2 | **Companion AI feels dumb** | High | High | Small behavior set (follow/hold/engage); player-triggered abilities; playtest early | Design |

| R6 | **Shoe teleport trivializes traversal** | Medium | Medium | Teleport only to valid ground; hazards/walls block; design gates around it | Level design |
| R7 | **Engine/console export friction** (Godot) | Medium | Medium | Validate early; consider porting partner; PC first | Tech |
| R8 | **Art pipeline bottleneck** (small art team) | High | High | Modular kit-bash; shared rig; stylized = forgiving; contract support | Art |
| R9 | **Voice/audio cost** | Medium | Medium | Bark system + partial VO; text-first; contract VO | Audio |
| R10 | **Burnout / key-person dependency** | Medium | High | Document systems (this vault); cross-train; realistic milestones | Production |
| R11 | **Accessibility afterthought** | Medium | High | Accessibility checklist per milestone; test profiles early | Design |
| R12 | **Narrative tone drifts to grimdark** | Medium | Medium | Pillar check: wonder + dread, never gore; writing guide | Writing |
